# Project essentials

## Mission
Build an Africa-first AI Sales OS that converts inbound business inquiries into tracked leads, appointments and opportunities through approved messaging integrations and human oversight.

## Model
Hybrid done-for-you agency plus multi-tenant self-service SaaS on shared platform infrastructure.

## Geography
First launch Ghana; prioritize real estate. Expand vertically only after validated pilot performance. Regional expansion requires provider, payments and legal readiness checks.

## MVP
WhatsApp Business Platform, web chat, verified knowledge base, configurable sales agent, lead CRM, scheduling, staffed handoff, audit trails and usage dashboards.

## Non-goals for MVP
Autonomous refunds, unsupervised financial transactions, bulk unsolicited messaging, unrestricted model database access, custom foundation model training, multi-country launch or native voice calling.

## Operating principles
Tenant isolation by default; customer consent; accurate verified answers; human approval for material actions; clear source-of-truth inventory; measurable outcomes; margin-aware unit economics.

## Decision gate
Do not start live campaigns before legal, provider and security prerequisites are met. Do not promise a sales uplift without baseline measurement.
