# AI Sales OS

Africa-first, multi-tenant AI sales platform with a managed agency delivery model and self-service SaaS.

**Status:** Architecture and planning starter kit; not yet a functioning application.

## Mission
Capture customer inquiries across authorized business messaging channels, qualify leads, arrange appointments, and hand conversations to human teams with auditability and tenant isolation.

## First release
Ghana; real estate design partners; WhatsApp Business Platform, web widget, AI agent configuration, knowledge base, lead CRM, bookings, human handoff, and analytics.

## Non-negotiables
Default-deny tenant-scoped access; webhook verification and idempotency; consent-aware messaging; human approval for sensitive actions; audited AI tool calls; safe secret management; reproducible tests and release gates.
