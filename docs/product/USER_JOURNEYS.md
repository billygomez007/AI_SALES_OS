# User journeys

## Managed business onboarding
Sales discovery > contract and data-processing terms > connect client-owned channel > import approved knowledge > configure agent > simulate > human review > supervised go-live > weekly outcomes review.

## Self-service onboarding
Create account > verify identity > create organization > choose template > connect approved channel > upload FAQs/inventory > run test conversations > publish under limits > view inbox/CRM/usage.

## End-customer inquiry
Inbound message > verified intent and inventory lookup > answer with approved facts > qualify interest > propose appointment > validate time slot > confirm via scheduling service > assign lead > staff follow-up > record outcome.

## AI to human escalation
Trigger (uncertainty, complaint, high-value decision, customer request) > queue/notify eligible staff > freeze automated replies where needed > show concise context > staff resolves > optionally return to approved automation mode.

## Offboarding
Provide exports where authorized, revoke provider connections, reconcile invoices, delete/anonymize in accordance with valid retention and legal-hold constraints.
