# Pricing hypotheses (GHS)

## SaaS
Starter GH₵499/month; Growth GH₵1,499/month; Business GH₵3,499/month; Enterprise custom. Included seats, channels, messages, AI usage and quotas must be explicitly set before billing goes live.

## Managed service
Launch GH₵3,000-6,000 setup plus GH₵1,500-2,500/month; Growth GH₵8,000-15,000 setup plus GH₵3,500-6,000/month; Enterprise GH₵20,000+ setup plus GH₵7,500+/month.

## Principles
These are experiments, not verified market prices. Separate third-party channel, inference and voice costs or impose transparent fair-use caps. Bill only from auditable usage records; provide overage alerts; support cancellation/export and documented refunds.

## Economics
Measure direct hosting + inference + message charges + support per tenant; target positive contribution margin before scaling acquisition.
