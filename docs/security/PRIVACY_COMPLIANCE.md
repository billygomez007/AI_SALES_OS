# Privacy and compliance implementation plan

## Ghana
Assess obligations under Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843), applicable Data Protection Commission registration requirements, marketing, retention, processor and cross-border rules with qualified counsel. Compliance status cannot be claimed from architecture documentation alone.

## Data governance
Maintain processing inventory, purpose/legal-basis records, notices, consent logs, controller/processor contracts, subprocessor register, retention schedule, data subject access/correction/deletion procedures and breach-response checklist.

## Messaging
Record lawful permission for outbound follow-up, honor opt-outs, minimize outreach and apply provider template restrictions. Keep transactional and marketing purposes separate.

## Regional
Complete individual reviews for Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa and any other target jurisdiction before activation; local payments, consumer marketing and call-recording rules also apply.
