# Delivery roadmap

## Phase 0: Validate (weeks 1-3)
25-30 interviews, three signed paid design partners, baseline metrics, pilot scope and pricing experiments.

## Phase 1: Foundation (weeks 4-10)
Tenancy, identity, roles, audit logs, WhatsApp/webhooks, unified inbox, AI agent, knowledge base, lead CRM, booking, handoff and usage dashboard.

## Phase 2: Managed pilots (weeks 11-16)
Supervised operations; evaluate response accuracy, bookings, consent, unit economics, escalation and client retention. Resolve all critical security findings.

## Phase 3: Self-service SaaS (months 5-6)
Self-serve onboarding, billing, quotas, standard templates, support flows and usage reconciliation.

## Phase 4: Growth (months 7-9)
Instagram/Facebook eligibility, payment links, additional vertical packs, quality evaluations and attribution.

## Phase 5: Expansion (months 10-18)
Voice and regional pilots only after Ghana margins, operational reliability and legal/provider review. Each phase has a go/no-go decision, not a guaranteed release date.
