# Go-to-market

## Beachhead
Ghana real estate agencies and developers receiving substantial inbound digital inquiries. Target decision-makers responsible for sales. Sell improved response coverage, qualified lead capture and appointment visibility, not guaranteed revenue.

## Discovery
Interview 25-30 prospects; measure inquiry sources, baseline response delays, missed leads, booking process, CRM maturity, willingness to pay and integration constraints.

## Offer
30-day paid managed pilot: business-verified knowledge, approved WhatsApp channel, supervised agent, CRM handoff, bookings, weekly performance review, scoped support and transparent metered costs.

## Acquisition
Founder outreach; live demo with realistic inventory; referrals; partnerships with agencies and CRM implementers; case studies with permission and properly attributed metrics.

## KPIs
Lead-to-meeting rate, median first response, human escalation success, pilot-to-paid conversion, acquisition cost, contribution margin and churn. Expand only after measured retention.
