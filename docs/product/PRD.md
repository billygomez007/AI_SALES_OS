# Product requirements document

## Problem
African businesses lose sales opportunities due to fragmented inquiries, delayed responses and inconsistent follow-up.

## Users
Business owner, sales manager, sales agent, agency implementation specialist, platform operator, inbound customer.

## MVP outcomes
Customer can ask a verified inventory question, be qualified, get a proposed appointment, receive a validated confirmation, and be handed to a person; owner can audit the journey and see lead attribution.

## Functional requirements
Organization onboarding; approved channel linking; knowledge ingestion/review; agent version/publish; inbox and assignment; contacts/leads/stages; calendars; consent-aware follow-up; delivery receipts; metric dashboard; usage metering; support controls.

## Nonfunctional requirements
Tenant isolation; accessible responsive UI; idempotent provider integration; observability; consent enforcement; configurable retention; provider failure fallback; safe shutdown; documented backup/rollback.

## MVP exclusions
Outbound bulk campaigns, self-authorized refunds, live voice AI, full advertising buying, broad marketplace integrations and healthcare advice.

## Acceptance
Pilot completes inquiry-to-booking workflow end to end with supervised agent and tested security gates. No sales-uplift claim without credible baseline and attribution.
